Most people call us here
First few nights

The bites can turn into more than bites

Some people react badly: swollen welts, blistering, hives. Scratching breaks the skin, which is how infections like impetigo or cellulitis get started.

After the first spray

One spray doesn’t end it

A surface spray kills what it touches and leaves the eggs behind. They hatch a week or two later and you are back where you started.

A few weeks in

They travel with your things

Clothes, bags, bedding, the sofa, a suitcase left in the hall. Take an infested bag to work and they come with you.

Two months in

They’re rarely where you look first

Not just the mattress. Behind the headboard, inside bed frame joints, under skirting boards, in furniture seams. Miss one and the rest of the work counts for nothing.

The longer it runs, the more of your home is involved. Finding out exactly where they are is the part that changes the outcome.

Bed bug treatment FAQ

What happens during the inspection?

A technician visits your home and checks mattress seams, bed frames and nearby furniture for signs of bed bugs. They explain the findings and any treatment needed.

Will heat treatment kill bed bug eggs too?

Heat treatment is designed to target both bed bugs and their eggs. Your technician recommends the treatment needed for your home and explains any follow-up visits.

Is the treatment guaranteed?

The guarantee and follow-up visits depend on your package. We’ll confirm the period, conditions and what’s included in your quote before you book.

Do I need to leave my home?

Yes. All people and pets need to leave during treatment. Your technician will tell you how long to stay away and when it’s safe to return.

How should I prepare for treatment?

You’ll receive instructions before the visit, including advice on bedding and belongings. Follow them carefully. Avoid moving items between rooms, as this can spread bed bugs.

Will I need another visit?

If needed, one follow-up spray visit may be available at no extra charge, usually around two weeks later. We’ll confirm what your treatment includes before you book.

How much does bed bug treatment cost?

The price depends on the affected area and the treatment needed. Ask for a free quote to see the cost and what’s included for your home.

Will I need a new mattress?

Often, no. Your technician can check whether your mattress can be treated before you replace it.

How soon can you come?

Same-day appointments may be available in London. Share your postcode so we can check the earliest suitable visit.

Can bed bugs come back after treatment?

Bed bugs can be brought into a home again. Follow your aftercare instructions and contact the team if you notice signs after treatment. They’ll explain the next step and any cover in your package.
